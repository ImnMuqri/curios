Questions owners ask

Is answering really free?

Yes. The AI answers your customers on every plan, for as long as you have a page. Business pays for the leads: the number, the chat, and the email that brings them to you.

What if it gives a wrong price?

It can only quote prices, dates and addresses that you approved. If nothing you approved answers the question, it tells the customer it will check with you. It never guesses, and never rounds a number.

Can it read my Instagram?

Not yet. Instagram and TikTok don’t let us read profiles. Paste your bio or a few paragraphs about your shop, or a website or Linktree link if you have one.

Does it reply in Malay?

It answers in the language the customer used: Malay, English, Manglish or a mix.

How do I pay?

By FPX online banking, e-wallet or card, through CHIP. RM39 a month or RM390 a year. Nothing is saved and nothing charges automatically.

What happens after my three free leads?

Your page keeps answering. To keep receiving leads, switch on Business.

Can I start with a personal page and switch later?

Yes, from Billing, whenever you start selling. It is a one-way switch: a business page can’t go back to personal.
