Questions owners ask first.

What if it gives a wrong price?

It can only quote prices, dates and addresses that you approved. If nothing you approved answers the question, it tells the customer it will check with you. It never guesses, and never rounds a number.

Is answering really free?

Yes. The AI answers your customers on every plan, for as long as you have a page. Business pays for the leads.

Can it read my Instagram?

Not yet. Instagram and TikTok don’t let us read profiles. Paste your bio or a few paragraphs about your shop instead.

Does it reply in Malay?

It answers in the language the customer used: Malay, English, Manglish or a mix.

How do I pay?

By FPX online banking, RM39 a month or RM390 a year. There is no card on file and no automatic charge.
