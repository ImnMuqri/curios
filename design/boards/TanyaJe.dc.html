Asked before. Answered here.

Can I use it for myself, not a business?

Yes. Personal is free: a page with an AI that answers questions about you, your work and your rates. It stays hidden from Google unless you switch it on.

What if it says something wrong?

It only answers from facts you approved. If nothing you approved answers the question, it says it will check with you. It never guesses, and never rounds a number.

Can it read my Instagram?

Not yet. Instagram and TikTok don’t let us read profiles. Paste your bio or a few paragraphs instead, or a website or Linktree link.

Does it reply in Malay?

It answers in the language the person used: Malay, English, Manglish or a mix.

Can I switch from Personal to Business later?

Yes, from Billing, whenever you start selling. It is a one-way switch: a business page can’t go back to personal.
